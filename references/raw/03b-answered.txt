  /btw what is the capital of Australia, and why isn't it Sydney? answer in 2 sentences

    The capital of Australia is Canberra. When the colonies federated in 1901, rival Sydney and Melbourne each refused
    to let the other be the capital, so a compromise was struck to build a new purpose-built capital between them,
    and Canberra was chosen and developed starting in 1913.
